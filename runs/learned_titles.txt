Continuous_MountainCarDynamicGoalEnv – a variant of Continuous Mountain Car where the goal position and goal velocity are sampled each episode and a small random wind force perturbs the dynamics. The agent receives a +100 reward for reaching the goal, a small step penalty, and an action‑magnitude penalty. Episodes end after 200 steps if not terminated.

Continuous Mountain Car with time‑varying terrain and a checkpoint reward
Create a new Gymnasium environment called Continuous_MountainCarDynamicWindCheckpointEnv. It should:
- Use the same observation (position, velocity) and action (continuous scalar) spaces as the base Continuous_MountainCarEnv.
- Sample a goal position uniformly in [0.45, 0.55] and a goal velocity in [0.0, 0.02] each episode.
- Apply a time‑varying wind force w(t)=A*sin(2π*t/period) where amplitude A ∈ [0.0,0.001] and period ∈ [50,150] are sampled at reset.
- Provide a checkpoint reward of +10 the first time the car crosses position -0.2 from left to right.
- Reward: +100 for reaching the goal (position ≥ goal_position and velocity ≥ goal_velocity), step penalty of -0.1*action^2, and the checkpoint bonus.
- Enforce a 200‑step episode limit internally (truncated flag).
